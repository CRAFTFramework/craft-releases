# Community

CRAFT for Claude Cowork is in **public beta**, and your experience shapes where it goes next.

## Where to talk

- **Discussions** — questions, ideas, show-and-tell, "how do I…". The friendliest front door.
- **Issues** — concrete problems: a download that won't verify, a broken link, a bug in a release.
- **Website** — guides, what's new, and the changelog at CRAFTFramework.ai.

## What we'd love to hear

- You're new to CRAFT — what was confusing in the first 10 minutes?
- A recipe or persona that helped (or didn't).
- Anything you wish existed.

Be kind and constructive (see [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)). This is a small,
human-run project — thoughtful feedback goes a long way.
