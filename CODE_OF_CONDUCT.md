# Code of conduct

This is a welcoming, beginner-friendly space. We want everyone — first-time users and
experienced builders alike — to feel comfortable asking questions and sharing feedback.

**Expected:** be kind, patient, and constructive. Assume good intent. Help newcomers. Keep
discussion on-topic and respectful.

**Not tolerated:** harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, or sustained disruptive
behavior. No spam or off-topic promotion.

If someone's behavior is making the space unwelcoming, or you have a concern, contact the
maintainer at conduct@craftframework.ai. Reports are handled privately and in good faith.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate this code, and may limit
participation for repeated or serious violations.
