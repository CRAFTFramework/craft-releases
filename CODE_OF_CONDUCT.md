# Code of conduct

CRAFT's community spaces — this repo's Issues and Discussions — exist so people can
report problems, test release candidates, and help each other. That only works if
everyone can take part without being harassed or belittled.

## The standard

**Be kind. Be specific. Assume good faith.**

Expected:

- Treat others with respect, including when you disagree.
- Keep feedback about the work — the release, the build, the docs — not the person.
- Be specific and reproducible; it's the most respectful thing you can do for a maintainer.
- Welcome newcomers and people still learning.

Not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Personal attacks, sustained disruption, or bad-faith argument.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.

## Scope

Applies to all participation in CRAFT community spaces — Issues, Discussions, and any
official CRAFT channel — and to anyone acting on the project's behalf in public.

## Reporting

If you see or experience behavior that breaks this code, contact the maintainer at
**conduct@craftframework.ai**. Reports are handled discreetly. For security or
signature concerns (not conduct), use [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, lock, or remove contributions, and may warn or ban accounts,
that violate this code. Decisions aim to keep the space safe and useful for everyone.
